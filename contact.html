Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I join Fitness Conscious Goa?

Simply fill out our contact form above or join us at one of our regular morning runs. We welcome fitness enthusiasts of all levels!

What fitness activities do you offer?

We organize morning runs, group workouts, beach fitness sessions, yoga classes, and various fitness challenges throughout the year.

Is there a membership fee?

Our community is free to join! Some special events or workshops may have nominal fees to cover costs, but regular activities are always free.

What should I bring to my first session?

Just bring comfortable workout clothes, running shoes, a water bottle, and your enthusiasm! We'll take care of the rest.
