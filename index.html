About Us

Promoting health, wellness, and an active lifestyle throughout Goa

Who We Are

Fitness Conscious Goa is a community of runners and fitness enthusiasts united by a common belief — that fitness has the power to transform lives. Founded on 1st January 2022 as a WhatsApp group, we grew into a movement. In April 2024, we were officially registered as a society under the Government of Goa (Registration No.: 87/2025).

Whether you're just starting your journey or you're a seasoned athlete, Fitness Conscious Goa welcomes everyone to be part of a growing culture that celebrates movement, discipline, and mental well-being.

Our Mission

To inspire every individual in Goa to adopt fitness as a lifelong habit by creating running events, fitness initiatives, and community programs that promote physical and mental well-being.

Our Vision

To build the largest fitness community in Goa, where every individual, regardless of age or fitness level, embraces an active lifestyle, making Goa one of India's healthiest and fittest states.

Our Journey

Past Activities

A look back at the events and initiatives that define who we are

01

Run for Cooperation – 7km

Madkai, Ponda, Goa

Successfully organized a 7 km run at Madkai in collaboration with Navdurga VKSS Society, Madkai, promoting fitness, community participation, and healthy living.

02

YARATHON 2025 – 7km Run

Farmagudi, Goa

Organized a 7 km run at Farmagudi to celebrate Friendship Day, in collaboration with JCI Farmagudi, encouraging people of all age groups to embrace an active lifestyle.

03

Donation Drive

Matrushaya, Ponda, Goa

Conducted a donation drive for Matrushaya as part of our commitment to giving back to society. This initiative reflected our belief that a strong community is built not only through fitness but also through compassion and social responsibility.

04

Bhagwad Geeta Shlok Chanting Competition

For School Students

Organized a Bhagavad Gita Shloka Chanting Competition for school students with the aim of reconnecting young minds with India's rich spiritual heritage. Studies suggest that regular recitation improves concentration, reduces stress, and instils moral values and discipline.

Looking Ahead

Our Future Plans

Building a healthier, stronger, and fitter Goa — together

Promote sports and physical activities among children from an early age.

Encourage every individual to adopt fitness as a lifelong lifestyle rather than a temporary goal.

Organize regular community runs, marathons, and fitness challenges across Goa.

Conduct fitness awareness programs in schools, colleges, and workplaces.

Build a strong network of volunteers, coaches, and fitness ambassadors.

Collaborate with government bodies, educational institutions, NGOs, and corporate organizations to promote public health.

Introduce fitness workshops, running clinics, and wellness seminars led by experienced athletes and fitness professionals.

Recognize and encourage outstanding athletes, volunteers, and individuals who contribute towards building a healthier Goa.

Promote environmental responsibility by organizing eco-friendly fitness events and awareness campaigns.

At Fitness Conscious Goa, we believe that fitness is more than exercise — it is a way of life. Our mission is to inspire people to move more, live healthier, and support one another in achieving their fitness goals. Together with our members, volunteers, and supporters, we are committed to creating a healthier, stronger, and more active Goa for present and future generations.

"Together, We Can Build a Healthier, Stronger, and Fitter Goa."
